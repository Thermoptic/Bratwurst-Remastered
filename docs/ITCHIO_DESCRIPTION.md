**Short description (tagline):**

## Description

**Bratwurst Reheated** is a fan-made remake of *Bratwurst* (1995), the Amiga space shooter originally fried by **Three Little Elks (3LE)**. Fly a vector spaceship through tight arenas, fight gravity and your friends, and be the last ship flying.

The goal of this project is to keep the feel of the original: the movement, the shooting, the crashes and the camera zoom that follows the action. On top of that come new ships, weapons, power-ups, maps, game modes and music.

### Play it your way
- **1 to 4 players** on one screen, with keyboard and Xbox-style gamepads.
- **Rounds and matches:** play several rounds (default 3). The last ship flying wins a round, and a results screen follows the last one, with rematch or back to menu.
- **Lives and respawn:** choose how many lives each player has. A destroyed ship respawns after a 3-2-1 countdown.

### Game modes
- **Classic:** the original deathmatch, last ship standing.
- **Astro Duel:** a space duel variant.
- **Asteroids:** shoot waves of rocks that split into smaller, faster ones. Set a LEVEL from 1 to 5 where every level doubles everything.
- *Race and Capture the Bratwurst are on the way.*

### Ships and weapons
- **15 vector ships**, each with its own handling, energy and look.
- Main cannon plus **special weapons**: Missile, Guided Missile, Turbo Boost, Tractor Beam and Cannon Turret. Switch special weapon and weapon position during the match.
- **Power-ups**, including the **Mini Nuke**, the charge-up **Railgun** (it fires by itself after three seconds) and **Drift**, a slide mode with light trails.
- Ships collide and knock each other away, and a boosting ship hits much harder. Sparks, explosions with expanding shockwaves and camera shake.

### Maps and map editor
- Three maps to start with: the original arena (**Sirius Bratwurst**) and two remixes (**Kepler Chorizo**, **Proxima Merguez**).
- **Map editor:** draw walls and boxes with the mouse, place start positions and save your own maps. New maps get a random star-or-planet-plus-sausage name.

### A camera that follows the action
The camera zooms in and out to keep every player in view, like the original. A **ZOOM option (1 to 10)** lets you zoom further out, up to twice as much of the arena. Navigation dots show where off-screen players are.

### Terminal look and a built-in music player
- A **command-deck / terminal style** interface: 30 dark colour themes (Base16), accent colours, scanlines and glow, sharp text at every resolution, and a wide playfield.
- Optional **solid colours**: fill ships and walls instead of outlines only.
- **Music player** in the main menu with a live spectrum, timeline, shuffle, repeat, volume and mouse control. A new song starts every round, and you can change song with LB / RB, even from the pause menu.
- Pause menu with match stats, controls and Options.
- Windowed 1x / 1.5x / 2x or fullscreen, VSync, and settings saved between sessions.

### Controls (gamepad)
Stick / D-pad turn, **A** cannon, **X / RT** thrust, **Y** special weapon, **B** power-up, **BACK** next special weapon, **LB** weapon pod, **LT + thrust** drift, **LT alone** reverse, **START** pause. Keyboard controls are supported for up to three players.
